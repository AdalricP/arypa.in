on your own terms

The helper is optional. Everything below is free, and none of it goes through anyone but you.

a model on your own computer

Free, offline, nothing leaves your machine.

  1. Install Ollama.
  2. Run ollama pull llama3.2 in a terminal.
  3. In swipelist, open settings and tap "helper".
  4. Pick "bring your own key". Leave the key empty, set url to http://localhost:11434/v1 and model to llama3.2.

For search that understands meaning as well as words, also run ollama pull nomic-embed-text.

your own key

Anything that speaks the OpenAI chat API works. DeepSeek is cheap and is the default.

  1. Make a key at platform.deepseek.com.
  2. On the helper page, pick "bring your own key" and paste it. A tick means it works.
  3. Keep url https://api.deepseek.com and model deepseek-chat, or use your provider's.

The key stays on your device. Notes only go to the url you set, and only while the helper is on.

your notes, your storage

Your notes are plain files on your computer: every note is a folder with a note.md. Syncing between devices isn't built yet.
