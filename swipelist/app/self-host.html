on your own terms

The swipelist plan pays for a model that runs the helper for you. You don't need it. Everything below is free, and none of it goes through me.

Run a model on your own computer

Free, offline, and nothing leaves your machine.

  1. Install Ollama.
  2. In a terminal, run ollama pull llama3.2.
  3. In swipelist, open settings, switch the helper on and tap "helper".
  4. Pick "bring your own key". Leave the key empty, set url to http://localhost:11434/v1 and model to llama3.2.

For search in the trash that understands meaning as well as words, also run ollama pull nomic-embed-text.

Use your own key

Any service that speaks the OpenAI chat API works. DeepSeek is cheap and is the default.

  1. Make a key at platform.deepseek.com.
  2. In the helper page, pick "bring your own key" and paste it.
  3. Leave url as https://api.deepseek.com and model as deepseek-chat, or swap in your provider's.

The key stays on your device. swipelist sends your notes only to the url you set, and only when the helper is on.

Keep your board on your own storage

Not built yet. Right now your board lives only on the device you're using. The plan is to let you pick a folder for it, so a Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox folder, or a server you sync with Syncthing, carries it between your devices without an account with me.
